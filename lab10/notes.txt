Lab 10 - notes.txt (Chiril-Adam Macari, FAF-243)

1. In "trace 32 grep hello README" why only read, not open or close?
   The kernel prints a call only if its bit is on in the mask. 32 = 2^5, so only
   bit 5 is on, and 5 is SYS_read. open is 15 and close is 21, their bits are off.

2. In forkforkfork, several pids print lines. Which line of Part 2 does that?
   np->trace_mask = p->trace_mask;   (kernel/proc.c, in fork)
   Every child copies the mask of its parent, so the children are traced too.

3. In "trace 65536 echo hi" the trace line is between "hi" and the new line. Why?
   echo calls write two times: write(1,"hi",2) and then write(1,"\n",1).
   The kernel prints the trace line right after each call returns, so the first
   line appears after "hi" and before the new line is written.

4. Which mask traces open (15) and close (21) and nothing else?
   2^15 + 2^21 = 32768 + 2097152 = 2129920.
   Tested: trace 2129920 grep hello README -> only "open -> 3" and "close -> 0".
